---

## 1. What Agency is not

Agency Portal must **NOT** be:

Admin Console with different colors.

---

## 2. Information architecture emphasis

Prioritize agency tasks such as:

- sales
- bookings
- customers
- requests
- action required
- commercial status

Foundation scope in P30 (`TC-P30-T009`): dashboard overviews — **not** full B2B rewrite.

---

## 3. Financial capability honesty

Only expose when corresponding domain capability is implemented and authoritative:

- Commission
- Credit
- Settlement
- Wallet
- similar financial concepts

**No fake Agency financial dashboards.**

---

## 4. Design System relationship

Agency shares Design System with Public/Admin but has its **own** workflow and information architecture.

Experience-specific components allowed. Do not force incompatible universal layouts.

---

## 5. Cross-cutting

- FA/EN/AR · RTL/LTR · Light/Dark
- Desktop / Tablet / Mobile independently reviewed
- trust and operational clarity over decorative charts
- preserve only real available business capabilities
